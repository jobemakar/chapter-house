# Feedback 03 — camera, clearer contraptions and pet feeding

2026-09-15. Selected plan before implementation. Supersedes Feedback 02's camera deferral and full-bowl re-tap behavior. Local application only; no publication.

## Brief

Make Wishbone Fling feel like a composed slingshot game: larger illustrated controls, a layered launcher, readable physical gadgets and a movable camera with real depth. Give the food bowl a complete fill/feed/empty loop. Keep the established unlimited-throw physics game, stable saves and earned rewards.

Mechanic signature remains pull/release, collide/topple/retrieve, discrete physics yards and cumulative recovery. Camera exploration changes presentation and controls, not the game loop or progression. It remains distinct from the registry's continuous steering, construction/routing, rhythm, matching, cooking and photography games. Wishbone is the source-book connection; gadgets, yards and feeding art are invented, spoiler-light game details.

## Numbered requirements

1. **F03-01 (user):** Restack and Keepsakes are larger icon-only illustrated buttons. Retain accessible names, keyboard focus and at least 44 CSS-pixel targets. Keep their existing actions and earned/locked keepsake details.
2. **F03-02 (user):** Replace the flat launcher with a stylized rotated wooden fork, visibly separated front/back arms, layered elastic/pouch and dog between the layers. Bands stretch to the held dog while aiming; recognizable shape and aiming clarity take priority over decoration.
3. **F03-03 (user):** User clarified: retain the upward-boost concept, replace unfamiliar bellows with a recognizable spring pad that compresses and rebounds. Render the magnet as a recognizable horseshoe with contrasting tips, with visibly metallic affected blocks. Match magnetic response to the visible material; do not imply wooden boxes are magnetic.
4. **F03-04 (user; default):** Simplify Teeter Tower and Domino Picnic by removing their incidental gadget and pickup clutter. Keep their physical piece order, target IDs, yard IDs and selection indices. These remain ordinary free-throw collapse puzzles. Focused spring/gate/magnet yards retain their designated mechanism.
5. **F03-05 (user):** Implement genuine pan, zoom and camera-relative parallax now. The current 1200×720 physics world is sufficient: overview shows the level, detail zoom makes the camera travel across it. Slower hills/trees and nearer fence layers move by different fractions of world-camera displacement. Time-based decorative drifting alone does not satisfy this requirement.
6. **F03-06 (controls default):** Drag from the launcher to aim; drag elsewhere to pan; wheel, pinch and visible zoom buttons change zoom, with a discoverable overview/home control. Camera follows flight and returns to the launcher; user exploration suspends follow until reset/next throw. Freeze camera during aiming. A second touch cancels aiming before pinch, and releasing a pinch never launches. Pause, dialog, resize, cancellation and disposal release gesture ownership. Camera-only exploration earns no active-play currency.
7. **F03-07 (user):** Empty bowl tap fills it. Full bowl tap sends one available pet along room navigation to eat, with a brief visible “nom nom” response, then leaves the bowl empty. Preserve food if no pet/path is available or approach is cancelled. Repeated taps cannot schedule overlapping meals. Editing still selects furniture; editing/removal/departure cancel the interaction cleanly. Each bowl persists its own filled state; feeding spends/earns nothing.
8. **F03-08 (carried):** Preserve coins, powers, keepsakes, rescue IDs, saves and once-per-run rewards. Keep TypeScript object-oriented responsibilities, mute and reduced motion. Camera state is transient and must not migrate physics coordinates. No loss of legacy checkpoint data because a mechanism is now hidden.

## Architecture and ownership

- **Root: camera.ts/game.ts.** Add a focused camera class owning world viewport center, bounded zoom, world-to-view and inverse view-to-world mapping, anchored zoom, pan and follow. Camera transforms use logical view units after CSS-to-canvas conversion; DPR affects raster resolution only. Retain full-world dimensions in physics/checkpoint validation. Game owns a pointer map and mutually exclusive aim/pan/pinch gesture state. Save a stable aim reference while camera is frozen; apply inverse camera coordinates to launcher hit tests and drag vectors. Zoom anchors the world point beneath the gesture midpoint. Clamp using viewport size at the current zoom. Start with overview zoom 1 and useful detail near 1.5; tuning is an implementation default.
- **Root: levels.ts/powers.ts and physics tests.** Explicitly set classic mechanisms/pickups to empty arrays instead of relying on current all-gadgets defaults. Preserve all classic pieces and target indices. Keep checkpoint gadget accounting fields (claimed, clearPaid, reward, gateOpen, polarity) intact even when bodies are omitted. Prefer additive material metadata or preserved kind semantics for metal art; no wholesale piece reorder/kind migration. Preserve focused mechanism activation and ordinary-shot completion.
- **Bounded art/UI agent: renderer.ts/ui.css.** Renderer receives the shared camera view and draws background layers with different camera factors, then transforms the world once for props, launcher, dog, trajectory, world particles and labels. DOM HUD remains fixed. Root owns game.ts markup and camera controls; coordinate renderer API before editing. Ensure background covers edges at all pans/zooms and reset canvas transforms before each frame. Layer the slingshot back arm/band, held dog/pouch and front band/arm in deliberate order. Render spring-pad feedback; retain internal bellows IDs for compatibility.
- **Bounded room agent: room.ts/profile.ts/main.ts plus relevant tests.** Reuse the existing trampoline pet approach/navigation and furnishing controller boundaries for an approach/eat lifecycle. Root does not edit those files concurrently. Begin consumption only after actual arrival, update that bowl's persisted state once eating completes, and release the pet back to roaming on completion/cancel. Clear transient action state on room disposal; keep existing per-instance bowl storage rather than adding global feeding state.

## Acceptance

1. Unit-check camera inverse mapping and pointer-anchored zoom at overview/detail, edge clamping and resize. Exercise mouse pan/wheel and two-pointer pinch; confirm second pointer and cancellation produce zero throws, and aim velocity remains consistent at different zooms. Camera exploration alone gives no coins.
2. Verify actual browser pan/zoom changes visible world framing, layer displacement differs observably, dog follow works at detail zoom, and return brings the launch area back. Check desktop, narrow portrait and tablet landscape for control overlap, icon recognition and launch access. Reduced motion removes smooth camera animation/shake without disabling manual navigation.
3. Simulate both classic yards without incidental gadgets, preserve an old partial checkpoint and reward-accounting round trip, and retain focused-yard mechanism tests and free-shot clear checks. Restack and reload cannot erase durable ownership or repeat a claimed reward.
4. Verify two bowls independently: empty tap fills; full tap routes pet, displays nom nom, ends empty; repeated tap, no pet, unreachable path, edit, removal and departure recover safely. Reload preserves completed filled/empty state and coins remain unchanged.
5. Run existing tests, strict TypeScript and production build; inspect final launcher layering, horseshoe magnet/metal blocks, spring pad and larger icon-only controls in-browser. Record actual simulation/browser evidence and physical-device/audio limitations. Update dated verification and shared plan/registry status without claiming unperformed playtests.
