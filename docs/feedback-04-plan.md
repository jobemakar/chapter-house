# Feedback 04 — simpler Fling and a walkable village

2026-09-15. Selected requirements and implementation boundaries recorded before code. Local application only; no Firebase, accounts, multiplayer, deployment or other-game integration. Current user feedback supersedes earlier pocket-power behavior and the earlier restriction on adding an outdoor space.

## Numbered requirements

1. Remove collectible pocket powers from active Wishbone Fling gameplay and UI completely: no pickups, activation buttons, power instructions, effects or new power discovery grants. Ordinary level mechanisms (lever, spring pad and metal-block magnet) remain. Preserve archived source saves, previously earned keepsakes, stable item IDs, owned inventory and durable reward accounting. Old pocket-power fields may remain inert compatibility data; they must not reactivate gameplay.
2. Rebuild the loaded slingshot silhouette to match the supplied reference: dog held in a visible leather pouch to the left of the fork, with a clearly readable face. Draw rear band, pouch/dog and front band/fork in deliberate depth order. Loaded, pulled, released and returning states must remain coherent.
3. Give dog-to-block impacts a short, restrained “kshh” sound with collision-strength gating and a cooldown to avoid a wall of noise. Keep mute, background suspension and teardown behavior. Do not trigger the new impact sound merely on ground contact or camera movement.
4. Add at least one genuinely wide authored level whose targets/structures extend beyond a normal launch-area viewport. Existing pan/zoom, flight follow and return should make its width observable and useful. Preserve existing level IDs, old partial checkpoints and ordinary-throw completion; adapt world bounds and camera inputs together. Unlimited throws and forgiving recovery remain.
5. Make avatar and pet reactions visually distinct using the user references: scalloped/cloud-like avatar bubbles and oval pet bubbles. Anchor them above the correct character, remain legible during movement, expire cleanly and support the existing petting/feeding and avatar actions. Avoid relying on text or color alone for the distinction.
6. Add a locally accessible outdoor village from the room with a clear return action. It is a scrollable, walkable scene larger than the viewport, reusing existing animal art and movement where suitable. Keep room state, avatar identity, pets, furnishing states and earned progression intact on round trips.
7. Compose an attractive cobbled village with shops/buildings, trees, planted details and a large central fountain that the avatar can walk all the way around. A stream provides a natural outer boundary. Trees, fountain and solid structures participate in navigation/collision; taps cannot route through water or solid scenery. These are invented cozy setting details, not claimed novel locations.
8. Tapping the fountain performs a free coin-flip/toss animation. It never spends or awards profile coins, creates a reward, or starts an idle-income loop. Prevent unlimited overlapping effects and provide a reachable interaction approach when needed.
9. Give the fountain a gentle looping water sound whose gain responds to avatar/listener distance and whether the fountain is visible in the camera. Pan away to attenuate it and return to hear it again. Smooth gain changes, obey the shared mute/background settings, unlock audio from a gesture and release the loop/context resources on departure.
10. Preserve touch and desktop controls, readable portrait/landscape layouts, reduced-motion behavior and recoverable navigation. Keep authored code TypeScript with focused classes and typed interfaces. Document actual automated/browser/audio verification separately from unperformed physical-device or speaker testing.

## Implementation and bounded ownership

Own `src/room/room.ts` and room reaction integration unless explicitly handed off. Reuse `RouteMotion` and the existing point/navigation conventions. Establish root-owned town contracts before dependent implementation. Root reviews game bounds/camera/save changes independently even when implemented in the bounded game branch.
- **Bounded game implementation agent (Terra):** Own `src/games/wishbone/**` and dedicated game tests. Remove runtime powers, redraw launcher, add dog/block impact audio and build a wide level with corresponding renderer/camera/world bounds. Do not edit main, core profile/catalog or room/town files. Report any required shared compatibility change to root. Preserve earned/archive data and supply focused checks for old saves, no new power grants, wide bounds and completion.
- **Bounded town art/audio agent (Terra):** Own only `src/town/art.ts`, `src/town/audio.ts` and `src/town/layout.ts` (plus an explicitly assigned focused test file if needed). Build an object-oriented Three.js village and procedural spatial water audio against root's agreed exports. Proposed composition is a 30×24 world, fountain centered at (15,10) with radius about 2, room-side entry (15,17), stream near z=20 and perimeter trees/shops. Treat dimensions as design defaults; report obstacle footprints and fountain position in one typed geometry contract shared by rendering and navigation. Export `TOWN` from `layout.ts` with width/depth/streamZ, entry, fountain and tree/building footprints. `TownArt` exposes `root: THREE.Group`, `update(dt, reducedMotion)`, `tossCoin()` and disposal; tag fountain/home pick meshes with `userData.kind`. `TownAudio(muted)` exposes `unlock`, `setMuted`, `setActive`, `setHidden`, `setView(visible, distance, pan)`, `coin`, `dispose` and `status`. No profile mutation, app routing or navigation ownership.
- **Reactions:** Root can implement a focused reusable `src/room/reactions.ts` with avatar/pet bubble kinds and connect it to existing rigs. A third bounded agent may own this only after explicitly agreeing nonoverlapping art/room files; no concurrent edits to the same files.

Implement the two independent bounded areas after this plan is saved while root builds integration. Integrate in small steps, inspect agent diffs, run targeted checks and perform final browser inspection. Keep historical docs intact; append dated implementation/verification and registry changes after the behavior is established.

## Acceptance checks

1. Load a pre-change profile with power discoveries and owned keepsakes, play/reload/return, and verify ownership and coin balance preservation. Search active UI/help and exercise gameplay to establish that powers/pickups cannot appear, activate or grant again. Existing mechanisms still work.
2. Inspect the launcher at rest and maximum pull at desktop and narrow widths: dog/pouch is visibly left of fork, face unobscured, bands join properly, launch input still maps correctly at multiple zooms.
3. Exercise a dog/block hit and repeated contacts with instrumented audio scheduling; ground-only impacts do not call the new recipe and mute/disposal silence it. Listen if an audio playback path is available; state the limitation if not.
4. Traverse the new level with overview/detail zoom and flight follow, confirming real distant structures, bounded panning, correct pointer-to-world mapping and return to launcher. Simulate ordinary-throw completion and retain classic/focused level tests.
5. Trigger avatar action, petting and feeding bubbles; visually confirm scalloped versus oval shapes, correct actor anchoring, expiration and no leaked scene objects after repeated transitions.
6. Enter town, walk around both sides of the fountain and several trees, try taps across stream/inside obstacles, pan to both ends and return home. Check persistent room/furnishing state and no stuck entry/exit. Inspect desktop, phone portrait and landscape.
7. Tap fountain repeatedly; observe a bounded free toss, no coin change and no durable reward. Verify water gain falls with distance and off-camera visibility, returns smoothly, and stops on mute, hidden page and scene disposal. Reenter repeatedly without accumulated voices/listeners/animation loops.
8. Run strict TypeScript, relevant automated tests and production build. Root independently reviews implementation and records actual browser, simulation, audio and device evidence in `docs/feedback-04-verification.md` before reporting completion.
