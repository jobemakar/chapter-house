# Picture Day Parade — revision plan

2026-09-10 plan · BOB-010-R2 · Implemented locally as 0.1.0 on 2026-09-12. See [requirements](requirements.md) and [verification](verification.md). Original proposal retained below.

Frame the funniest moment in a wonderfully chaotic school photo booth. Cue a harmless prop, catch a wobbling hat or a group pose, and snap a comic keepsake. Every shot gives a funny outcome; timing and framing reveal more combinations. No real camera or anxiety failure meter.

## Primary mechanic
Frame and time snapshots of playful animated photo scenes.

## Proposed placeable rewards
Framed game photos, camera lamp and photo-backdrop screen. These are original thematic extensions, not claimed literal objects from the book.

## Current versus planned
This direction is now the current playable. Numbered requirements were recorded before implementation. The original machine pitch remains historical. Current scope includes three scenes, nine timing/composition moments, optional kept prints and local keepsake ownership, with no album economy or public deployment.
