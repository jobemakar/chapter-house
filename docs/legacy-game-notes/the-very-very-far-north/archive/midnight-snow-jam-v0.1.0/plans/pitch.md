# Midnight Snow Jam — proposed concept

Date: 2026-09-10
Book: *The Very, Very Far North* by Dan Bar-el
Idea: BOB-004
Status: proposed only; no game source or playable has been generated.

Throw an Arctic jam session with Duane and a band of wonderfully odd animal friends. Tap snow drums, swipe shimmering notes, and add new instruments as the sky dances overhead. Miss a beat and the band keeps playing; finding the groove makes the whole snowy stage come alive.

## Mechanic signature
- Input: Tap broad beat pads and make short directional swipes; generous timing windows.
- Primary verbs: tap, swipe, layer, perform.
- Loop: Play a musical phrase → add an instrument or flourish → flow into the next arrangement.
- Spatial structure: A stationary musical stage; timed patterns rather than navigation.
- Recoverable friction: Misses soften a flourish without stopping the song or removing prior rewards; optional relaxed tempo.
- Progression: Band members, instruments, arrangements, stage lights.
- Art: Felt-and-paper Arctic theater, pale blues and gentle aurora ribbons.
- Audio: original or properly licensed music/effects with immediate mute; specific sound design to be selected before implementation.

## Distinctness
The only rhythm-led candidate. It shares audio with the other games but makes musical timing its primary input.

## Before implementation
If this concept is selected, write numbered requirements from the shared template, specifying controls, timing, recovery, visual/audio treatment, progression, save behavior, and acceptance checks. Do not treat this pitch as authorization to build the game.

## Potential shared-world reward
A little Arctic bandstand with an animated musician.
