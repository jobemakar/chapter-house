# Midnight Snow Jam requirements
2026-09-11 · BOB-004-R2 · selected by Codex under the user's instruction to choose one unbuilt idea and create a playable.

## Book, original work and distinctness
Verified publisher premise: Duane is a polar bear, Major Puff a puffin, Handsome a musk ox and Boo a caribou; friendship connects the Arctic cast. Source checked 2026-09-11: https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Very-Very-Far-North/Dan-Bar-el/The-Very-Very-Far-North/9781534433427 . The concert, instruments, melodies, nighttime scenery and keepsakes are original thematic inventions. No story scenes or illustrator artwork are reproduced.

Signature: tap four musical pads in time → play a short phrase → the ensemble grows → release an aurora flourish → continue through a three-tune set. Stationary stage, temporal coordination, forgiving misses that never end a run. Distinct from the collection's steering, routing, collapse, cooking, merging, stealth, habitat building, containment and photo timing. Rhythm remains the central action rather than a cosmetic accompaniment.
