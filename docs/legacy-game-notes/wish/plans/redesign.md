# Wishbone’s Big Fetch: Backyard Ruckus — revision plan

2026-09-10 · BOB-003-R2 · redesign-planned. Not implemented.

Lob a sock into a wobbly stack of boxes and cushions. The toy hits first; Wishbone barrels after it, sending the structure tumbling and freeing squeaky toys. Keep the mess and throw again until every toy is free.

## Primary mechanic
Aim a toy projectile to trigger dog pursuit and physics collapse of authored structures.

## Proposed placeable rewards
Rolled-sock toy, ball basket, dog bed and doghouse side table. These are original thematic extensions, not claimed literal objects from the book.

## Current versus planned
The 0.1.0 demo, source and saves remain unchanged. [Next requirements](requirements-next.md) govern the proposed replacement.
