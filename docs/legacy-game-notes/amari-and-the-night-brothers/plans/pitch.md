# Bureau After Dark — proposed concept

Date: 2026-09-10
Book: *Amari and the Night Brothers* by B. B. Alston
Idea: BOB-007
Status: historical proposal, superseded by the user-selected two-floor sigil/riddle mystery on 2026-09-17. See [current requirements](requirements.md). Preserved below as the original brief, not a description of the current implementation.

Slip through a wonderfully strange supernatural bureau after hours, disguising yourself as a lamp, a plant, or something much less convincing. Sneak past wandering magical oddities and recover escaped enchanted objects. Getting spotted causes a comic magical mishap and opens a detour, rather than restarting the mission.

## Mechanic signature
- Input: Tap a destination; tap large disguise buttons; drag a distraction into place.
- Primary verbs: sneak, disguise, distract, retrieve.
- Loop: Observe patrols → choose a disguise/path → retrieve an object → adapt to the next room.
- Spatial structure: Connected top-down rooms with patrol routes and hiding spots.
- Recoverable friction: Detection causes a short, recoverable disguise mishap or detour; recovered objects stay recovered.
- Progression: Disguises, enchanted artifacts, bureau departments.
- Art: Luminous indigo graphic novel, gold seals, whimsical magical silhouettes.
- Audio: original or properly licensed music/effects with immediate mute; specific sound design to be selected before implementation.

## Distinctness
Unlike Pocket Funhouse, the challenge is timing around agents and choosing disguises, not manipulating room geometry. No scrolling flight/dash loop.

## Before implementation
If this concept is selected, write numbered requirements from the shared template, specifying controls, timing, recovery, visual/audio treatment, progression, save behavior, and acceptance checks. Do not treat this pitch as authorization to build the game.

## Potential shared-world reward
A levitating artifact cabinet and a mischievous lamp.
