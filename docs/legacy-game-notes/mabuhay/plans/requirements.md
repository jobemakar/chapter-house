# Moonlight Munch Run requirements

Updated 2026-09-17; authorized local shooter evolution (parent plan 42).
Earlier harmless-bump/manual-toss design is preserved in Git history and
superseded by Jobe's explicit shooter choices. No publication.

1. Canonical standalone local game, separate from Chapter House. Midnight
   Merienda v0.1.2 remains an intact runnable archive and its
   `midnight-merienda-v1` and `midnight-merienda-settings-v1` saves are not read.
2. Spoiler-light Beautiful Pig cover details anchor generated comic art. All
   creatures, scenery, feeding, bosses and rewards are original inventions.
3. Travel continuously right through a scrolling landscape; free 2D steering
   with mouse, WASD/arrows or left-origin touch drag. Food fires forward automatically.
4. Catch pickups to switch straight/spread/piercing servings. Duplicate catches
   strengthen that type; every type retains its levels. Rapid fire stacks separately.
5. Three spaced waves per stage, progressively tougher patterns and later side
   entry. Larger guests need more hits; no shot-type matching is required.
6. Contact, escaped hungry guests and boss projectiles deplete food supplies.
   At zero, pause for free Restock & continue; preserve upgrades, earned feeds,
   keepsakes, current wave/stage and remaining boss hunger. Ordinary wave replays.
7. Boss stops road scrolling while steering remains active; aimed projectiles
   and summoned guests. Feed boss to resume travel and advance the stage.
8. Rechargeable special-serving burst: Space/F, right touch or separate button;
   steering and special input may be simultaneous. Ordinary firing is free.
9. Generated locally packaged scenery and sprite atlases, actual frame animations,
   original WebAudio with safe fallback, mute, pause, help, reduced motion and fullscreen.
   Wide road uses 80% playfield; landscape preferred, portrait letterboxed.
10. TypeScript object-oriented source, reproducible offline build, MIT adapted
    routines with full notice, legacy-key migration and bounded storage sanitizer.
11. 2026-09-17 challenge feedback (parent plan 43): creature-dropped road hazards
    cannot be removed with food or special servings. A warned, active contact
    interrupts automatic and special firing for one simulation second. No extra
    supply loss; old food continues. Hazards are transient and cleared on restock.
    More urgent wave pacing and boss attacks retain crowd caps/free continuation.

Mechanic signature: 2D positioning / catch / feed / burst; horizontal travel;
read waves, catch upgrades, feed boss, continue journey; supplies/restock recovery;
retained weapon and keepsake progression. User deliberately selected scrolling
and shooting overlap; this remains a standalone comparison, not integrated rewards.
