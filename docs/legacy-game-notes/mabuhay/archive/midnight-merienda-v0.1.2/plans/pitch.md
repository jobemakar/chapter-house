# Midnight Merienda — proposed concept

Date: 2026-09-10
Book: *Mabuhay!* by Zachary Sterling
Idea: BOB-005
Status: proposed only; no game source or playable has been generated.

Run a dazzling food truck where the evening crowd gets increasingly magical. Drag ingredients, flip sizzling treats, and juggle several cooking stations while charmingly strange customers gather for a feast. A mix-up creates a silly special instead of ending the shift.

## Mechanic signature
- Input: Drag ingredients to stations and plates; tap/short swipe to finish actions.
- Primary verbs: assemble, cook, plate, serve.
- Loop: Read pictured orders → coordinate stations → finish and serve dishes → unlock festival bustle and new combinations.
- Spatial structure: A compact multi-station food truck counter.
- Recoverable friction: Orders wait or can be remade; missed timing reduces a bonus, not the run. No impatient-customer failure meter.
- Progression: Recipes, truck customizations, friendly customers, festival locations.
- Art: Vivid graphic-novel color, warm market lights, appetizing hand-drawn food.
- Audio: original or properly licensed music/effects with immediate mute; specific sound design to be selected before implementation.

## Distinctness
Unique resource scheduling and assembly loop. Unlike Gummy Galaxy, ingredients form orders rather than merge through a combinatorial board.

## Before implementation
If this concept is selected, write numbered requirements from the shared template, specifying controls, timing, recovery, visual/audio treatment, progression, save behavior, and acceptance checks. Do not treat this pitch as authorization to build the game.
