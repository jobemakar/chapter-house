# Pocket Funhouse — proposed concept

Date: 2026-09-10
Book: *The Mystery of Locked Rooms* by Lindsay Currie
Idea: BOB-002
Status: original pitch selected on 2026-09-10; local 0.1.0 demo implemented. See requirements.md and design.md for the actual demo scope.

A miniature funhouse fits under your fingertips. Slide walls, spin entire rooms, and set off satisfying chains of secret doors, moving platforms, and hidden treasure compartments. There are several routes forward, so one stubborn mechanism never traps the whole adventure.

## Mechanic signature
- Input: Drag room parts; rotate with a large on-screen handle; tap mechanisms.
- Primary verbs: slide, rotate, connect, reveal.
- Loop: Manipulate room geometry → trigger physical mechanisms → reveal a route or treasure → explore another room.
- Spatial structure: A dollhouse of connected, inspectable rooms.
- Recoverable friction: Misaligned parts are freely reversible; alternate open rooms and optional mechanism nudges prevent forced stalls.
- Progression: Mechanical curios, expanding funhouse wings, alternate routes.
- Art: Cut-paper theatrical dioramas, jewel colors, brass machinery.
- Audio: original or properly licensed music/effects with immediate mute; specific sound design to be selected before implementation.

## Distinctness
Different from Popcorn's machine construction: these are authored mechanisms to manipulate and uncover, not player-built production chains. No riddles, trivia, or typed answers.
