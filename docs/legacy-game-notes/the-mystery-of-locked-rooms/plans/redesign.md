# Pocket Funhouse: The Wandering Key — revision plan

2026-09-10 · BOB-002-R2 · redesign-planned. Not implemented.

Guide a little wind-up key through a miniature funhouse by rearranging blocks, conveyors, pressure plates and bridges. The joy is making a physical chain of events work; a wrong turn keeps the key wandering while your construction stays intact.

## Primary mechanic
Rearrange platforms and mechanisms to guide an autonomous wind-up key to a lock.

## Proposed placeable rewards
Mechanism worktable, wind-up key and secret-door bookcase. These are original thematic extensions, not claimed literal objects from the book.

## Current versus planned
The 0.1.0 demo, source and saves remain unchanged. [Next requirements](requirements-next.md) govern the proposed replacement.
