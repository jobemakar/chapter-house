# Feedback 02 — interactive furnishings and focused Wishbone yards

2026-09-15 · Selected implementation plan, written before code. Astra planning and final integration review; bounded UI/art work delegated to a less expensive model. Local application only; preserve standalone Wish demos and publication identities.

## Brief and decisions

Make the room feel playful through three interactive furnishings, make the shared spending unit unmistakably a gold coin, and give Wishbone Fling a single game-first screen containing five authored puzzles. The established dog launch, cumulative destruction, unlimited retries and earned keepsakes remain the mechanic. Wishbone is the verified source-book dog; these yards, furnishings and contraptions are original inventions connected loosely to companionship, not purported novel scenes.

Mechanic signature: pointer pull/release; launch, collide, topple, retrieve; discrete authored physics puzzles; challenge is finding a useful point of impact; misses retain damage and ownership; unique toy discoveries and throws award permanent keepsakes. This remains distinct from Stormglide continuous steering, Funhouse/Contraption Club construction and routing, rhythm, cooking, matching, stealth, habitat care, containment and photography. A five-yard content pass is selected; unlimited random yards and mandatory consumable gates are rejected.

## Numbered functional requirements

1. **F02-01 Bowl (user):** Add an ordinary purchasable pet food bowl. Tap a placed bowl to visibly fill it with food. Filling is a free playful interaction, never a care obligation or currency source. Persist filled state per furniture instance; re-tapping a full bowl may give a brief response without subtracting food or money. Editing continues to select/move it instead of feeding it.
2. **F02-02 Aquarium (user):** Add an ordinary purchasable fish tank with clearly visible swimming fish, water and glass. Tap to make its fish dart faster for a short bounded interval, then resume calm swimming. Multiple tanks have independent animation clocks/responses. Reduced motion minimizes idle swimming and replaces the dart with a restrained visible response. This furnishing is separate from owned companion pets.
3. **F02-03 Trampoline (user, interaction default):** Add an ordinary purchasable pet trampoline. A tap invites an available home pet to a short playful bounce at the trampoline and then returns it to roaming. Show a clear response if there is no pet. Stop/restore cleanly when editing, removing furniture or leaving the room. Do not teleport a pet through obstructing furnishings or leave its logical navigation position stuck on the prop.
4. **F02-04 Currency (user concern, selected design):** Label the shared currency **Coins**, using an original round gold coin with rim and inset mark, not a sparkle, four-point star, emoji dependent on platform or three-diamond motif. Update wallet, shop prices, insufficient-funds text and game rewards consistently. Preserve numeric balance, activity accrual, prices and saved key; this is a display rename, not an economy conversion.
5. **F02-05 Single game surface (user):** While in Wishbone, remove Chapter House branding and redundant outer shell/game headers. Keep one compact overlay HUD: Clubhouse exit, Wishbone/yard identity, Coins, sound, fullscreen, help and pause. Reuse shared capabilities, avoiding duplicate controls. The canvas dominates available space on desktop, phone and tablet; controls remain at least 44 CSS px and do not obscure launch or important targets.
6. **F02-06 Game tools (user):** Restack and Keepsakes become visible illustrated/game-styled stage-overlay buttons. Keepsakes stays discoverable, opens existing earned/locked collection details, and retains source thresholds. Powers move into a compact expandable game overlay with counts and an accessible label; Gust remains available in flight. Do not bury keepsakes inside settings.
7. **F02-07 Recall (selected design):** Existing code automatically begins return after a settled flight or at 4.2 seconds, with a 1.2-second return. Retain the useful optional shortcut as **Recall**, visible only during flight/return, with help text “Wishbone comes back automatically. Recall brings him back sooner.” Hide it when ready. It must neither restack nor lose confirmed progress/unused wind. Explain this actual purpose rather than retain unexplained “Call Wishbone.”
8. **F02-08 Slingshot (user):** Draw a recognizable forked wooden launcher, two elastic bands meeting the held plush/pouch, and visibly stretched bands while aiming. Keep the launcher behind the dog, a clear launch zone and high-contrast trajectory cue. Preserve relative pointer input, touch cancellation, pause and resize behavior. Artwork must convey the pull direction without a wall of text.
9. **F02-09 Five puzzles (user):** Offer the five new yards below as the main set, all freely selectable, with clear ordinal/name and toy count. Each has one dominant cause-and-effect idea and can be cleared using unlimited ordinary throws without powerups. Levers, magnets and bellows are per-yard opt-in definitions, not automatically installed in every yard. Optional collectible powerups remain banked and never gate completion.
10. **F02-10 Recovery and migration (carried):** Keep original yards, stable target IDs, earned items, checkpoints, currency and power inventory. Existing partially completed yards reopen correctly. Restack starts only a fresh transient run in that yard and preserves durable awards. Power pickup and clear rewards remain once per run, including reload. Update misleading “all eight toys” threshold copy to “eight different toys” without changing threshold or item ID.
11. **F02-11 Presentation/accessibility (carried):** Preserve mute, pause/dialog/input ownership, readable labels, visible collision feedback in mute mode and reduced-motion behavior. New room animations stop on disposal/background and do not grant active-play coins. Reuse current original art/audio vocabulary; no new remote dependencies or audio assets required.

## Scrolling/parallax decision

Keep these five puzzles within the current complete-yard viewport for this pass. A larger scrolling world changes pointer-to-world mapping, camera follow/return, offscreen target discovery and save validation, while the requested clarity can be solved with authored layouts and a larger unobstructed canvas. The existing 4.2-second flight cap also limits meaningful distant content. Record larger scrolling yards with slower background parallax as **deferred after five-yard playtest**, not implemented and not rejected. Camera movement must earn its complexity with a level requiring it; a decorative pseudo-scroll is not completion of larger levels. Do not silently claim scrolling support.
