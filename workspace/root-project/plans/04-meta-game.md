## Historical revision follows

2026-09-10 · META-001-R2 · Planning only.

Jobe selected an **isometric 3D space with placeable collected items, including objects on furniture**. The Storyship remains a leading fiction; ship versus treehouse has not been decided.

## The important interaction
Place a table beside a window. Put Wishbone's toy and a Funhouse keepsake on it. Move or rotate the table: both objects follow. Pick either object up independently and put it on a shelf. Furniture provides real support surfaces.

Every collectible has a placeable form and a documented thematic link, which may be very loose. Scores/XP/currency remain accounting values rather than collectible objects.

## No avatar yet
No character setup, movement controls, roaming companions or pathfinding actor. Store floor areas, obstacles, entrances and object heights so later walking would be possible. Preserve door clearances and offer an editor path overlay; do not build avatar gameplay.

## Existing broad proposals retained
Shared XP, guaranteed themed rewards, optional cosmetic spending, no lost progress, no mandatory streaks, and access to all available games remain useful principles. Economy and level pacing are uncommitted until the core loops are fun. Ownership and surface placement are the first things to validate.

META-001 Storyship, META-002 Pocket Planet, and META-003 Secret Treehouse are retained in [the earlier brainstorm](archive/04-meta-game-before-revision-2.md). The isometric presentation and surface-placement requirements supersede its flat-room suggestions and old reward examples.

## First room, when built
One room, one table, a shelf and a few themed objects. Validate touch placement, stacking, carrying contents with furniture, undo, save/reload and future walking clearance. No backend, provider account, multiplayer or avatar is required for that experiment.
