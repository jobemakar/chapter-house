## Decision for the current three-demo review
Keep Pocket Funhouse's name: “pocket” is an honest format description, just as “Big Fetch” names our game activity. Tighten its collectibles to original keys, mirror fragments, panels and secret-passage hardware. Its rewards are not a catalogue of objects taken from the book. Keep Wishbone as the recognizable emotional anchor for Big Fetch. Stormglide remains a fantasy inspired by lightning and shelter dogs, with no new changes in this build.

## Future gate
Before implementing another pitch, identify at least one sourced, spoiler-light story anchor that affects play or the player's emotional connection. Separate verified book details, thematic interpretation, and invented game material in the requirements. A cover color, title pun or renamed token alone is weak justification. Do not turn the games into quizzes to create a connection.
