# Willowbrook stream activities and mobile pinch — 2026-09-15

Jobe selected two forgiving shared-world activities for the Chapter House town. Fishing is available only from a clear stream bank and follows cast → ripple → persistent Reel → result, with no timing failure and an approximately 30 percent catch chance resolved only when reeling. Digging is available anywhere walkable, briefly shows soil and a held shovel, and never leaves a permanent hole. Misses cost nothing.

Six fish and eight finds occupy separate sections of one persistent Collection view. Discoveries have common, uncommon and rare tiers; duplicates stack as counts; locked entries show silhouettes and rarity hints. The fish cards use a small CC0 subset of Kenney Fish Pack, while tools and world effects remain procedural. Cartoony activity cues share the existing mute, hidden-page and disposal lifecycle.

The same selected pass adds true midpoint-anchored two-finger zoom to both Willowbrook and the clubhouse, including cancellation of accidental taps/walks, and gives narrow screens a closer clubhouse default. This is a meta-world discovery layer, not an additional book game; Wishbone Fling's launch/topple/retrieve signature is unchanged.

Astra planned and integrated the profile migration, world/UI/animation, camera gestures and final browser review. Bounded GPT-5.6 Terra work supplied the deterministic activity core/tests, town audio/tests and Kenney asset audit/packaging. All 80 tests and the production build pass. Desktop and 390×844 emulated-touch checks cover the full prompt/cast/reel loop, collection layout and both pinch cameras. No physical-device, account, multiplayer or publication claim is made.
