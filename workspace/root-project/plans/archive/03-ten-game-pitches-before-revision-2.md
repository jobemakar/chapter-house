# Ten books, ten different games

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: Stormglide implemented; Pocket Funhouse and Wishbone’s Big Fetch now have local demos; seven concepts remain proposed. Original pitch text is retained below. See 08-book-connections.md for verified anchors and weak-link flags.

Book titles and authors were transcribed from Jobe’s supplied photo. These are loose, spoiler-light game inspirations, not claims that the invented game events occur in the books. All concepts are touch-first, entertainment-only, and forgiving; no quizzes or questions posed to the player.

## BOB-001 — Stormglide

Book: *The Miscalculations of Lightning Girl* — Stacy McAnulty

Surf a tiny cloud through painted skies, scoop up ribbons of sparks, and gather a flying pack of rescue pups. Weave through rings or burst through grumpy clouds; every bump is just a wobble, and the adventure keeps going.

- **Core mechanic:** Free-flight collection and timed dash.
- **Touch interaction:** Relative thumb steering plus a second-thumb dash; keyboard and mouse remain optional.
- **Visual treatment:** Twilight gouache, soft cream type, mint and rose light.
- **Mistakes:** Brief slowdown and combo friction; score and discoveries remain.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A tiny storm-cloud couch and a pup companion.
- **Overlap check:** Existing baseline. No new candidate uses its scrolling steer/collect/dash loop.

[Book reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/557145/the-miscalculations-of-lightning-girl/) · [Book folder](../../the-miscalculations-of-lightning-girl/README.md)

## BOB-002 — Pocket Funhouse

Book: *The Mystery of Locked Rooms* — Lindsay Currie

A miniature funhouse fits under your fingertips. Slide walls, spin entire rooms, and set off satisfying chains of secret doors, moving platforms, and hidden treasure compartments. There are several routes forward, so one stubborn mechanism never traps the whole adventure.

- **Core mechanic:** Tactile mechanical room puzzles.
- **Touch interaction:** Drag room parts; rotate with a large on-screen handle; tap mechanisms.
- **Visual treatment:** Cut-paper theatrical dioramas, jewel colors, brass machinery.
- **Mistakes:** Misaligned parts are freely reversible; alternate open rooms and optional mechanism nudges prevent forced stalls.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A secret-door bookcase that actually opens.
- **Overlap check:** Different from Popcorn's machine construction: these are authored mechanisms to manipulate and uncover, not player-built production chains. No riddles, trivia, or typed answers.

[Book reference](https://www.penguin.com.au/books/the-mystery-of-locked-rooms-9781728259536) · [Book folder](../../the-mystery-of-locked-rooms/README.md)

## BOB-003 — Wishbone’s Big Fetch

Book: *Wish* — Barbara O'Connor

Pull back a frisbee and send it skipping through a sunlit backyard full of ramps, wind chimes, and ridiculous ricochets. Wishbone chases every throw, even the gloriously bad ones. Discover trick-shot routes and turn an ordinary game of fetch into a joyful stunt playground.

- **Core mechanic:** Slingshot-style trick throws.
- **Touch interaction:** Drag to aim and set power; release to throw.
- **Visual treatment:** Warm watercolor summer, chalk trails, paper-textured gardens.
- **Mistakes:** Misses become goofy fetch animations; the disc always returns, with no lives or reload screen.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** Wishbone’s doghouse and a frisbee toy.
- **Overlap check:** Unlike Stormglide, movement is not continuously steered. Unlike Popcorn, the player directly launches each projectile rather than constructs a machine.

[Book reference](https://clubs.scholastic.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-master-catalog-rco-us/default/dw8c54950d/pdfs/wish-discussion-guide.pdf) · [Book folder](../../wish/README.md)

## BOB-004 — Midnight Snow Jam

Book: *The Very, Very Far North* — Dan Bar-el

Throw an Arctic jam session with Duane and a band of wonderfully odd animal friends. Tap snow drums, swipe shimmering notes, and add new instruments as the sky dances overhead. Miss a beat and the band keeps playing; finding the groove makes the whole snowy stage come alive.

- **Core mechanic:** Forgiving rhythm performance.
- **Touch interaction:** Tap broad beat pads and make short directional swipes; generous timing windows.
- **Visual treatment:** Felt-and-paper Arctic theater, pale blues and gentle aurora ribbons.
- **Mistakes:** Misses soften a flourish without stopping the song or removing prior rewards; optional relaxed tempo.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A little Arctic bandstand with an animated musician.
- **Overlap check:** The only rhythm-led candidate. It shares audio with the other games but makes musical timing its primary input.

[Book reference](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Very-Very-Far-North/Dan-Bar-el/The-Very-Very-Far-North/9781534433427) · [Book folder](../../the-very-very-far-north/README.md)

## BOB-005 — Midnight Merienda

Book: *Mabuhay!* — Zachary Sterling

Run a dazzling food truck where the evening crowd gets increasingly magical. Drag ingredients, flip sizzling treats, and juggle several cooking stations while charmingly strange customers gather for a feast. A mix-up creates a silly special instead of ending the shift.

- **Core mechanic:** Food-truck assembly and station juggling.
- **Touch interaction:** Drag ingredients to stations and plates; tap/short swipe to finish actions.
- **Visual treatment:** Vivid graphic-novel color, warm market lights, appetizing hand-drawn food.
- **Mistakes:** Orders wait or can be remade; missed timing reduces a bonus, not the run. No impatient-customer failure meter.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A snack cart that serves your clubhouse visitors.
- **Overlap check:** Unique resource scheduling and assembly loop. Unlike Gummy Galaxy, ingredients form orders rather than merge through a combinatorial board.

[Book reference](https://clubs.scholastic.com/mabuhay%21/9781338738605-rco-us.html) · [Book folder](../../mabuhay/README.md)

## BOB-006 — Gummy Galaxy

Book: *Not If I Can Help It* — Carolyn Mackler

Drop, stretch, and squish jewel-colored gummy shapes into a satisfying little world. Matching gummies combine into increasingly delightful creations, and gentle chain reactions reveal new shapes. There’s always a way to make room—no overflowing-board game over and no noisy surprise effects.

- **Core mechanic:** Squishy merge-and-shape sandbox.
- **Touch interaction:** Drag and drop shapes; pull large stretch handles; tap a free scoop tool.
- **Visual treatment:** Translucent candy glass, clean aqua and peach palette, calm optional sound.
- **Mistakes:** Crowding can be cleared with a free scoop/rearrangement; earned discoveries remain.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A squishy gummy beanbag and translucent lamps.
- **Overlap check:** The only merge/discovery board. It does not model sensory processing differences as a defect, challenge meter, or thing to cure; the candy inspiration is a loose cover motif.

[Book reference](https://www.scholastic.ca/our-books/book/not-if-i-can-help-it-9780545709484) · [Book folder](../../not-if-i-can-help-it/README.md)

## BOB-007 — Bureau After Dark

Book: *Amari and the Night Brothers* — B. B. Alston

Slip through a wonderfully strange supernatural bureau after hours, disguising yourself as a lamp, a plant, or something much less convincing. Sneak past wandering magical oddities and recover escaped enchanted objects. Getting spotted causes a comic magical mishap and opens a detour, rather than restarting the mission.

- **Core mechanic:** Disguise-based playful stealth.
- **Touch interaction:** Tap a destination; tap large disguise buttons; drag a distraction into place.
- **Visual treatment:** Luminous indigo graphic novel, gold seals, whimsical magical silhouettes.
- **Mistakes:** Detection causes a short, recoverable disguise mishap or detour; recovered objects stay recovered.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A levitating artifact cabinet and a mischievous lamp.
- **Overlap check:** Unlike Pocket Funhouse, the challenge is timing around agents and choosing disguises, not manipulating room geometry. No scrolling flight/dash loop.

[Book reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/742781/amari-y-los-hermanos-de-la-noche--amari-and-the-night-brothers-by-bb-alston/9781644738757/) · [Book folder](../../amari-and-the-night-brothers/README.md)

## BOB-008 — Sanctuary Seasons

Book: *The Elephant in the Room* — Holly Goldberg Sloan

Turn a little patch of land into an elephant’s favorite place. Shape pools, plant shade, build muddy play areas, and watch the elephant invent its own games with what you create. Each new arrangement changes how the habitat behaves and attracts more tiny visitors.

- **Core mechanic:** Habitat-building simulation.
- **Touch interaction:** Brush terrain/water, drag habitat pieces, tap to observe interactions.
- **Visual treatment:** Clay-and-watercolor miniature landscape, lush greens and warm terracotta.
- **Mistakes:** A poor arrangement is freely movable; dry areas and blocked routes can be repaired, with no animal injury or permanent losses.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A miniature elephant fountain and a leafy balcony.
- **Overlap check:** Overlaps intentionally with the meta layer's placement interface only. This game's core challenge is a responding habitat system; clubhouse decoration is cosmetic expression. Different from Wildfire's reactive containment.

[Book reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca/books/554551/the-elephant-in-the-room-by-holly-goldberg-sloan/9780735229952) · [Book folder](../../the-elephant-in-the-room/README.md)

## BOB-009 — Emberwatch

Book: *Wildfire* — Rodman Philbrick

Command a tiny forest-rescue crew from above. Send water drops, open safe corridors, and outmaneuver drifting cartoon embers as the wind changes. The map is always moving, but setbacks become smoky detours and regrowth opportunities—not a lost campaign.

- **Core mechanic:** Real-time containment strategy.
- **Touch interaction:** Tap hotspots to assign water crews; drag broad protective lines and safe routes.
- **Visual treatment:** Stylized papercraft forest, ember orange against teal water, clear readable map.
- **Mistakes:** Smoke temporarily blocks an area; tools replenish and the forest can recover. No playable child faces a death state.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A glowing camp lantern and a regrowing miniature forest.
- **Overlap check:** Unique pressure-containment and resource-priority loop. Unlike Sanctuary Seasons, success depends on reacting to a moving situation, not long-term habitat construction. Fictional arcade abstraction, not survival instruction.

[Book reference](https://www.scholastic.ca/our-books/book/wildfire-9781338266900) · [Book folder](../../wildfire/README.md)

## BOB-010 — Popcorn Contraption Club

Book: *Popcorn* — Rob Harrell

Build the world’s least sensible popcorn machine. Place fans, funnels, trampolines, and conveyor belts, then watch kernels bounce into a spectacular chain reaction. Keep rearranging the machine while it runs; escaped popcorn circles back for another try.

- **Core mechanic:** Build-and-tinker chain-reaction machines.
- **Touch interaction:** Drag machine parts; tap to rotate; move pieces while the simulation runs.
- **Visual treatment:** Loose comic ink, buttery yellow, blue doodles, elastic cartoon motion.
- **Mistakes:** Spills automatically recirculate; unlimited editing and undo, with no destruction of collected rewards.
- **Possible shared-world reward:** A working miniature popcorn machine for the clubhouse.
- **Overlap check:** Unlike Wish's direct trick shots, the player designs a reusable machine; unlike Pocket Funhouse, the player assembles new mechanisms rather than solves an authored room. Anxiety is not represented as a failure meter or joke.

[Book reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouseretail.com/book/?isbn=9780593697948) · [Book folder](../../popcorn/README.md)
